Benin Kingdom (Edo)

One of West Africa's most renowned states, famed for its bronze and ivory art, palace and long dynastic history.

Origins & development

Oral traditions trace the Oba dynasty to Ife connections, with Benin City becoming a major urban and political centre. The kingdom reached its height in the 15th–16th centuries under rulers who expanded territory and patronised the arts.

Art & urbanism

Benin's brass plaques, sculptures and ivory carvings are celebrated worldwide. The palace complex and its guild system reflected sophisticated organisation and royal patronage.

European contact

Benin engaged in controlled trade with Europeans from the late 15th century. Diplomatic exchanges and tensions shaped relations until the British punitive expedition of 1897.
