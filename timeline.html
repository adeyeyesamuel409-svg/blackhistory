African History Timeline

From ~20,000 BP to the present, key developments linked to UNBROKEN pages.

Prehistory (c. 20,000–5,000 BP)

  • c. 20,000–10,000 BP — Origins & early peopling: Africa as the cradle of humanity.
  • c. 10,000–5,000 BP — Holocene shift: fishing, herding and early settlement.

Ancient (c. 3000 BCE–700 CE)

  • c. 3100 BCE — Kemet (Ancient Egypt): unified state.
  • c. 760 BCE–650 CE — Nubian kingdoms: Napata, Meroë.
  • c. 300 BCE–300 CE — Aksum emerges.

Medieval & Early Modern (c. 700–1800 CE)

  • c. 700–1600 CE — Ghana, Mali, Songhai.
  • c. 1235 CE — Mali Empire rises; Mansa Musa reigns.
  • c. 1070–1890 CE — Kanem-Bornu.
  • c. 1100–1500 CE — Great Zimbabwe.
  • c. 1000–1500 CE — Indian Ocean trade & Swahili coast.
  • c. 1500–1900 CE — Queen Njinga of Ndongo-Matamba.
  • 869–883 CE — The Zanj Rebellion.

Slavery & Resistance (c. 1500–1900)

  • c. 1500–1860s — Transatlantic slave trade.
  • 1720s–1730s — Nanny of the Maroons (Jamaica).
  • 1 March 1896 — Battle of Adwa.

Colonial & Independence (c. 1880–Present)

  • c. 1884–1960 — Scramble & colonial rule.
  • c. 1957–1980 — Independence era.
  • Present — Living culture, Knowledge & innovation, Myths debunked.
