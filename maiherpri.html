Maiherpri

Nubian noble buried in KV36, Valley of the Kings (c. 1418-1390 BCE), reign of Thutmose IV.

Identity and dating

Maiherpri (c. 1418-c. 1390 BCE) was an ancient Egyptian noble of possibly Nubian origin buried in tomb KV36 in the Valley of the Kings. He lived during the reign of Thutmose IV.

Titles and role

His titles included "Child of the Nursery" and "Fan-bearer on the Right Side of the King". The fan-bearer title placed him close to the pharaoh as an advisor or bodyguard. The "Child of the Nursery" title may indicate he was raised in the royal nursery, possibly as a prince of a vassal territory.

Burial and significance

Buried in KV36 in the royal necropolis, Maiherpri's tomb contained a well-preserved mummy and grave goods. His status demonstrates Nubians held positions of influence at the Egyptian court during the 18th Dynasty.
