Exceptions worth knowing

Ethiopia was never colonised — it crushed an Italian invasion at Adwa in 1896, though it was occupied from 1936 to 1941.

Liberia, founded by the American Colonization Society, declared independence in 1847 and is Africa's oldest republic.

South Africa was a self-governing dominion from 1910, but only won full democracy with the end of apartheid in 1994.

Western Sahara remains a disputed, non-self-governing territory, and Réunion is an overseas department of France.
