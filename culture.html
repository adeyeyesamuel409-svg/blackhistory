Cloth, music & memory

Culture as living knowledge

Textiles

Cloth as language

Kente motifs carry proverbs; adire and bogolan are dyed and painted with meaning; Ndebele wall paintings mark home and identity. Patterns encode history, status and belief — cloth you can read.

Orature

The griot's memory

Mande jeliw (griots) carried genealogies, law and history in song, accompanying themselves on the 21-string kora. Oral tradition was a rigorous archive, kept accurate across generations.

Music

Rhythm as structure

Polyrhythm, call-and-response and improvisation within a shared frame shaped music across the continent — and, through the diaspora, much of the modern world's music.

Rites & philosophy

Masks, rites & Ubuntu

Gelede masquerades honour mothers and community; Ifa divination frames fate and ethics; Ubuntu — “I am because we are” — puts relationship at the centre of personhood.
