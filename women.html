Women in African History

Women have shaped politics, resistance, scholarship and culture across African history. This hub connects key figures with verifiable sources.

Queen Njinga (c. 1582–1663)

Ndongo-Matamba ruler who resisted Portuguese colonisation in Angola.

Empress Taytu Betul (c. 1851–1918)

Strategic leader at the Battle of Adwa (1 March 1896).

Yaa Asantewaa (c. 1840–1921)

Led the War of the Golden Stool against British rule (1900).

Nanny of the Maroons (c. 1686–c. 1750s)

Akan leader who secured Maroon autonomy in Jamaica.

Agojie of Dahomey

Documented all-female regiments of the Kingdom of Dahomey.
