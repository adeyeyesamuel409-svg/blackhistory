The beginnings in Africa

The oldest fossils of our species, Homo sapiens, are African. From the continent, humans dispersed in waves that eventually reached every habitable landmass on Earth — long before any wheel, road, or ship.

The evidence

Older, and wider, than once thought

For most of the 20th century, the “cradle” was placed in East Africa. New finds pushed the timeline back and spread it across the continent.

  • Jebel Irhoud, Morocco — c. 315,000 yrs
  • Omo Kibish, Ethiopia — c. 233,000 yrs
  • Blombos Cave, South Africa — c. 100,000 yrs
Why it matters

A shared human origin

Genetic and fossil evidence agrees: every living human population traces its ancestry to Africa. Migration out of the continent is the family tree of all of us.

Note on language: “Out of Africa” describes population movement, not a single tidy departure. It was many movements over tens of thousands of years.
