About

Why UNBROKEN

An independent, non-commercial educational project. Its goal is simple: tell the long African story accurately, beautifully, and accessibly — with sources you can check.

Our principles

  • Evidence first. Every claim is indexed in Sources.
  • Honest about debate. Contested topics are labelled, not hidden.
  • African agency. People are shown as makers of history, not only its victims.
  • Accessible. Free, fast, mobile-first, and usable without an account.

Roadmap

Volume I — Origins & Empires (live): Origins, Kemet & Nubia, Kingdoms, Culture.

Volume II — Trade & the slave trades: trans-Saharan, Indian Ocean and transatlantic routes; volumes, agency and resistance, using the Slave Voyages archive.

Volume III — Colonialism & independence: the Berlin Conference, wars of resistance, and decolonisation.

Get involved

Know a better source? Spotted an error? Corrections are welcome and will be credited. Share the project — visibility is what keeps independent history alive.
