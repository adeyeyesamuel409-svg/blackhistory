Test Yourself

5 quick questions based on Myths debunked.

  1. Africa had no writing before colonialism? Answer: False. (e.g. Meroitic, Ge'ez, Ajami, Timbuktu manuscripts)
  2. Great Zimbabwe was built by outsiders? Answer: False. Archaeology shows Shona builders.
  3. Ethiopia was colonised? Answer: False. Ethiopia defeated Italy at Adwa (1896).
  4. Trans-Saharan trade didn't exist? Answer: False. It connected West Africa to North Africa for centuries.
  5. Africa is a country? Answer: False. It's a continent of 54 countries with diverse histories.
